Where we step in

Common fractional CFO questions.

What does a fractional CFO do for an owner-led business?

A fractional CFO turns accounting information into forward-looking cash-flow forecasts, management reporting, financing plans, and decision support so the owner can see what the business can afford and what needs attention next.

Can a fractional CFO help prepare for business financing?

Yes. We connect historical results, operating assumptions, working capital, debt service, and downside cases in a lender-ready forecast and financing package.

What makes HS different from a generalist fractional CFO?

Alongside business forecasting, financing readiness, acquisitions, and exit planning, HS brings specialist capability in development pro formas, construction cash flow, project funding, lender draws, and real estate finance.
