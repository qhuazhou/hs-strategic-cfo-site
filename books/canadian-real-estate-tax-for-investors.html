What the book helps you decide

Tax planning across the full property lifecycle.

The book is organized around investor decisions rather than isolated tax definitions.

Before you buy

Compare personal, joint and corporate ownership; decide who should be on title; understand acquisition taxes; and connect the financing structure to future interest deductibility.

While you own

Work through rental income, deductible costs, CCA, renovations, refinancing, short-term rentals, vacancy rules and changes between personal and rental use.

When you sell

Distinguish capital gain from business income, estimate recapture and sale tax, consider principal-residence choices, and understand additional non-resident procedures.

A decision framework

Good tax planning preserves future options.

A deduction today can create recapture later. A title decision can make a future transfer expensive. Refinancing can preserve or impair interest deductibility depending on how the borrowed money is used. A change of use can produce a deemed disposition.

The book explains these connections so investors can identify the question before signing, transferring, refinancing, claiming or selling.
