The Canadian Real Estate Tax Decision Series · Book 3

When Tax Events Happen

Transfers, Death, Trust Events, Restructuring, Development and Cross-Border Changes.

By Calvin Zhou, Ph.D., CPA

A tax event can happen even when no conventional sale occurs.

A transfer, death, change of use, reorganization, trust distribution, development decision, involuntary disposition or change of tax residence can force the tax system to remeasure ownership, value, income or tax basis. This book helps readers recognize the trigger while elections, valuations and transaction choices are still available.

Four records

Document the event before memory replaces evidence.

For every material change, preserve the event date, governing fair market value, tax basis immediately before the event, and the election or reporting deadline.

  • Identify what fact changed.
  • Determine what is recognized, deferred or withheld.
  • Map remaining elections, rollovers and valuations.
  • Show the cash or liquidity consequence.
  • Coordinate accounting, tax and legal implementation.
