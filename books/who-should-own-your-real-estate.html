A practical rule

Complexity is worthwhile only when it solves a real problem.

A structure may defer one tax cost while weakening financing, adding compliance, complicating distributions or limiting a later exit. The right comparison starts with the property, investors, debt, cash-flow plan and expected transition.

  • Identify legal and beneficial ownership.
  • Map guarantees, control and economic rights.
  • Model how cash reaches each investor.
  • Test how capital enters and exits.
  • Confirm implementation with tax and legal professionals.
