Selling, Transferring and Passing On a Private Company.

Enterprise value is not what the owner actually keeps.

Between a headline price and durable personal wealth sit debt, working capital, tax, escrow, earnouts, vendor financing, rollover equity and retained risk. This owner-side guide explains the decisions behind selling, transferring, recapitalizing or passing on a Canadian private company.

Exit readiness

A better exit begins before the transaction.

Reliable financials, defensible normalized earnings, qualified shares, clean working capital, transferable operations and a realistic transition plan require lead time. The book turns those issues into an owner decision sequence.

  • Bridge enterprise value to equity value and cash at closing.
  • Compare QSBC and LCGE planning with deal certainty.
  • Test earnouts, vendor financing and rollover equity.
  • Plan for family succession, estate freezes and equalization.
  • Reduce founder dependency before a buyer prices it.
