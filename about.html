Advisory approach

Make the financial work usable. Forecasts, models, financing packages, reports, and transaction analysis should reconcile and support the next owner decision.

What this means for clients

You get a forecast, model, financing package, report, or advisory process that connects the numbers to the decision and deadline in front of the owner.

  • If growth is consuming cash faster than expected, the forecast should show when and why.
  • If financing depends on assumptions, working capital, and debt service, the model should make them clear and defensible.
  • If the decision involves an acquisition, exit, property, or development project, the analysis should connect business cash flow to transaction and project risk.
